# Operational Example: Bay to Shanshui Circuit

## Required Images

- Source blueprint: `../assets/shanshui-before.png`
- Transformation reference: `../assets/shanshui-after.png`

Always view both images and provide both to the image-generation tool. A path written in this file does not make the image visible to the model.

## What the Example Actually Preserves

- original viewpoint and horizon;
- mountain ridge rhythm and its position around the bay;
- the bay as the central opening;
- the foreground railing as a continuous directional line;
- near–middle–far depth.

## What the Example Completely Rebuilds

- mountain → translucent colored soft-body landform;
- sea → saturated interactive liquid core;
- railing → the only continuous amusement circuit;
- vegetation → abstract gel or mineral supporting masses derived from existing planting;
- ground and infrastructure → coherent structural surfaces;
- daylight → luminous premium 3D environmental light.

The result is connected to the source through topology, not through unchanged photographic texture.

## Visual Roles

- The source image controls composition.
- The result image controls transformation strength and finish.
- The hero is the transformed mountain body.
- The liquid core creates emotional color depth.
- The circuit organizes movement through the world without becoming the only designed element.

## Failure Comparison

Reject:

- the original bay with a white walkway added;
- unchanged mountains and plants behind a transparent installation;
- a generic glass city unrelated to the bay;
- literal Chinese architecture or decorative flowers;
- a dense amusement park containing many small rides.

Approve:

- a complete new 3D world;
- broad readable masses;
- major source zones transformed together;
- clear source topology when viewed beside the before image;
- fresh, controlled, luminous color and premium material depth.
