# Source-Specific World Families

Select one family only after reading the source. A family is a starting grammar, not a preset.

## Folded Urban Garden

**Use for:** city, civic complex, blue-hour architecture, transit edge.  
**Keep legible:** skyline, architecture, roofline, plaza, vertical urban rhythm.  
**Hero:** folded ceramic/enamel building body or softened structural block.  
**Field:** reflective glazed plaza, smoky atmospheric gap, or quiet water plane.  
**Circuit:** folded along rooflines, stairs, escalators, facade seams, or bridges.  
**Materials:** pearl ceramic + satin metal + small translucent accent.  
**Avoid:** turning towers into mountains; all-glass sci-fi city.

## Tide Mineral Theatre

**Use for:** rocky beach, rough coast, cliff, surf, overcast shore.  
**Keep legible:** mineral mass, tide direction, sand/rock collision, horizon.  
**Hero:** porous stone, wet mineral, polished shell, or broad chalk body.  
**Field:** tide membrane, shallow colored water, or misty ocean field.  
**Circuit:** embedded groove, tide channel, or restrained edge path that enters and disappears.  
**Materials:** mineral stone + liquid membrane + ceramic or metal accent.  
**Avoid:** full elevated boardwalk and cheerful resort architecture.

## Canopy Column Theatre

**Use for:** palm beach, trees, trunks, planted avenue, vertical vegetation.  
**Keep legible:** trunk spacing, canopy rhythm, vertical growth, open field beyond.  
**Hero:** woven membrane canopy, milky resin column, ceramic bark abstraction, or mesh growth structure.  
**Field:** sand and sea as two contrasting horizontal fields.  
**Circuit:** threaded between columns, wrapped around trunks, or partially concealed in canopy shade.  
**Materials:** woven mesh/membrane + glazed stone or milky resin + liquid field.  
**Avoid:** literal palms left unchanged, fake mountains, generic boardwalk.

## Cloudy Terrain Reservoir

**Use for:** mountain, garden slope, green ridge, inland water, layered planting.  
**Keep legible:** landform rhythm, layered depth, water opening, foreground edge.  
**Hero:** one translucent colored soft-body terrain or cloudy mineral hill.  
**Field:** liquid blue reservoir, mist opening, or glazed basin.  
**Circuit:** suspended span or ridge-hugging loop derived from the existing line.  
**Materials:** cloudy gel/mineral resin + liquid field + pearl ceramic circuit.  
**Avoid:** turning every leaf and support into translucent gel.

## Enamel Ground Machine

**Use for:** concrete plaza, industrial edge, roads, retaining walls, construction sites.  
**Keep legible:** seams, slabs, structural repetition, ramps, machinery rhythm.  
**Hero:** enamelled slab, satin-aluminum block, glazed concrete fold.  
**Field:** reflective ground, fog pocket, or colored material basin.  
**Circuit:** follows joints, ramps, rails, conveyor logic, or embedded grooves.  
**Materials:** enamel + satin metal + mesh/luminous accent.  
**Avoid:** introducing nature where the source offers a stronger industrial language.
