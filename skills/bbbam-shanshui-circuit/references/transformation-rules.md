# Shanshui Circuit Transformation Rules

## 1. Fixed Style, Variable Source Mapping

Always build the same B.B.BAM visual system. The source only decides which existing shapes become the hero, field, circuit, and support.

Retain:

- camera, crop, horizon, perspective, relative placement, scale relationships, silhouette rhythm, and near–middle–far organization;
- semantic anchors: city stays urban, mountain stays landform, rocks stay mineral, palms stay canopy-column rhythm, water stays a field.

Replace:

- all photographic textures and local surface detail;
- major geometry with a few continuous translucent soft-body equivalents;
- original infrastructure with one source-derived pearl circuit ribbon.

Transform the full visible world, not a selected object.

## 2. Map Five Source Zones

| Source zone | What to identify | Fixed-style translation |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | largest or most characteristic body | 1–3 connected, smooth colored translucent formations |
| Field | sky, sea, sand, plaza, or open void | one broad bright liquid, mist, pearly plane, or light core |
| Circuit | railing, shore, road, stair, roofline, trunks | one continuous milk-pearl ribbon with long radius turns |
| Supports | repeated towers, rocks, plants, columns | 1–3 quiet broad translucent volumes; never micro-objects |
| Signal | a small warm highlight in source or composition | one peach/coral/amber/lilac focus |

Transform at least hero, field, and circuit together.

## 3. Morphological Echoes

- **City:** tower clusters become a few smooth translucent vertical architectural bodies; rooflines/stairs/escalators become one pearl ribbon circuit; central civic roof can become the broad soft hero. Never make the skyline into mountains or every facade into a window grid.
- **Mountain/green slope:** ridge becomes 2–5 layered jade/tea continuous soft terrain shells; water/open sky becomes cyan liquid/light core; foreground plants become one smooth translucent growth blanket or a few broad soft mounds, never individual leaves, stems, blobs, or capsules.
- **Rock coast:** rock masses become one or two mist-blue/seafoam smooth mineral bodies; surf/sea becomes a calm bright liquid plane; sand becomes one pale warm pearly ground plane; the shore or rock edge becomes a restrained pearl ribbon that enters and disappears.
- **Palm beach:** trunks become a limited number of tall smooth translucent columns; canopies become broad rounded transparent membrane discs; sea becomes a liquid core; beach becomes a warm pearly plane; trunk spacing or shoreline becomes the pearl ribbon. Distant city stays a calm pale urban silhouette, never a mountain range.
- **Plaza/industrial edge:** slabs and structural grids become a few clear soft architectural volumes; seams/ramps become the circuit; open ground becomes one liquid or pearly field.

No major new mass may lack a source counterpart.

## 4. Prompt Contract

Use this structure:

> “Image 1 is the source blueprint: preserve its camera, crop, horizon, topology, relative scale, source palette, and [semantic anchors]. Image 2 is the fixed B.B.BAM Shanshui Circuit style reference: use its full-world transformation strength and its luminous material grammar — continuous colored translucent soft-body volumes, one milk-pearl ribbon circuit, and a broad bright liquid/mist core. Ignore any UI or text in Image 2.
>
> Rebuild more than 80% of Image 1 as one complete, luminous, premium C4D world. Map [hero] to [1–3 continuous translucent hero forms], [field] to [one broad liquid/mist/pearl plane], and [source line] to [one smooth pearl ribbon circuit]. Transform [supports] into only [number] quiet broad translucent equivalents. Use [source-led palette]. Preserve original category logic; do not retain photographic texture. Large-scale, sparse, calm, thick smooth surfaces with long unbroken highlights and controlled caustics. Absolutely no fragments, facets, leaf fields, pebbles, bubbles, capsules, droplets, crystalline detail, grain, cellular textures, or dense supports. It must read as the same B.B.BAM series as the reference at thumbnail size.”

## 5. Rejection Tests

Reject if any is true:

- hiding the circuit reveals a largely unchanged photo;
- the output does not visibly share the reference's continuous-soft-body / pearl-ribbon / luminous-core material grammar;
- source categories have been changed without cause;
- the output adds unrelated flowers, plants, mountains, pagodas, dragons, or random props;
- the route becomes a generic white S-shaped boardwalk unrelated to a source line;
- the composition has many equal elements and no dominant hero;
- any large surface is faceted, tiled, scaly, rippled with noisy detail, granular, or crowded by small translucent pieces;
- vegetation is rendered as many leaf-like objects, stalks, drops, capsules, or blobs instead of one continuous soft growth mass;
- it is too dark, too realistic, too metallic, too matte, or too cluttered.
