# Shanshui Circuit Transformation Rules

## 1. Fixed Style, Variable Source Mapping

Always build the same B.B.BAM visual system. The source only decides which existing shapes become the hero, field, circuit, and support.

Retain:

- camera, crop, horizon, perspective, relative placement, scale relationships, silhouette rhythm, and near–middle–far organization;
- semantic anchors: city stays urban, mountain stays landform, rocks stay mineral, palms stay canopy-column rhythm, water stays a field.

Replace:

- all photographic textures and local surface detail;
- major geometry with translucent soft-body equivalents;
- original infrastructure with a source-derived pearl circuit.

Transform the full visible world, not a selected object.

## 2. Map Five Source Zones

| Source zone | What to identify | Fixed-style translation |
|---|---|---|
| Hero | largest or most characteristic body | one dominant colored translucent soft-body formation |
| Field | sky, sea, sand, plaza, or open void | bright liquid, mist, pearly ground, or light core |
| Circuit | railing, shore, road, stair, roofline, trunks | a continuous milk-pearl route with the same direction |
| Supports | repeated towers, rocks, plants, columns | quiet translucent secondary volumes |
| Signal | a small warm highlight in source or composition | one peach/coral/amber/lilac focus |

Transform at least hero, field, and circuit together.

## 3. Morphological Echoes

- **City:** tower clusters become translucent vertical architectural bodies; rooflines/stairs/escalators become pearl circuits; central civic roof can become the soft hero. Never make the skyline into mountains.
- **Mountain/green slope:** ridge becomes layered jade/tea soft terrain; water/open sky becomes cyan liquid/light core; foreground plants become simplified translucent growth masses, not literal leaves.
- **Rock coast:** rock masses become mist-blue/seafoam soft minerals; surf/sea becomes bright liquid; sand becomes pearly warm ground; the shore or rock edge becomes a restrained pearl route that appears and disappears.
- **Palm beach:** trunks become tall translucent columns; canopies become broad soft translucent membranes; sea becomes liquid core; beach becomes warm pearly ground; trunk spacing or shoreline becomes the circuit. Distant city stays a pale urban silhouette, never a mountain range.
- **Plaza/industrial edge:** slabs and structural grids become clear soft architectural volumes; seams/ramps become the circuit; open ground becomes liquid or pearly field.

No major new mass may lack a source counterpart.

## 4. Prompt Contract

Use this structure:

> “Image 1 is the source blueprint: preserve its camera, crop, horizon, topology, relative scale, source palette, and [semantic anchors]. Image 2 is the fixed B.B.BAM Shanshui Circuit style reference: use its full-world transformation strength and its luminous material grammar — colored translucent soft-body volumes, milk-pearl circuits, and a bright liquid/mist core. Ignore any UI or text in Image 2.
>
> Rebuild more than 80% of Image 1 as one complete, luminous, premium C4D world. Map [hero] to [translucent hero], [field] to [liquid/mist/pearl field], and [source line] to [pearl circuit]. Transform [supports] into quiet translucent equivalents. Use [source-led palette]. Preserve the original category logic; do not retain photographic texture. Few large forms, one strong hero, clear bright air, controlled caustics. It must read as the same B.B.BAM series as the reference at thumbnail size.”

## 5. Rejection Tests

Reject if any is true:

- hiding the circuit reveals a largely unchanged photo;
- the output does not visibly share the reference's translucent-soft-body / pearl-circuit / luminous-core material grammar;
- source categories have been changed without cause;
- the output adds unrelated flowers, plants, mountains, pagodas, dragons, or random props;
- the route becomes a generic white S-shaped boardwalk unrelated to a source line;
- the composition has many equal elements and no dominant hero;
- it is too dark, too realistic, too metallic, too matte, or too cluttered.
