# B.B.BAM Shanshui Circuit

Rebuild the whole photographed environment as one coherent, enterable artificial shanshui world. Preserve the source's spatial identity, not its photographic surfaces.

## Mandatory Reference Protocol

Before generating:

1. Load and inspect `assets/shanshui-after.png` with the available image-viewing tool. Do not rely on its filename or the text description alone.
2. Assign the images these roles:
   - **User source:** structural truth for camera, framing, horizon, relative positions, scale relationships, and silhouette rhythm.
   - **Target example:** truth for transformation strength, world completeness, material clarity, color luminosity, and premium 3D finish.
3. Pass both the user source and the target example to the image-generation tool. Instruct the tool to rebuild the user's composition using the example's transformation grammar without copying the example's exact layout.

If the target example cannot be loaded or supplied to the image-generation tool, state the limitation before generating. Never silently fall back to a text-only interpretation.

## Global Rebuild Contract

Preserve only:

- camera position, framing, horizon, and perspective;
- relative placement and scale of major source zones;
- silhouette rhythm and the semantic relationship among foreground, hero mass, water or void, and distance.

Rebuild:

- the geometry and material identity of every major zone;
- terrain, planting masses, water, rocks, ground, railings, roads, architecture, and environmental lighting;
- the entire image as a premium C4D-like spatial world.

At least three major source zones must undergo structural transformation. The hero landform, the liquid or void field, and the circuit must all be visibly transformed when they exist. Do not preserve original photographic vegetation, rock, concrete, facade, sand, or water texture merely to maintain recognizability.

## Spatial Hierarchy

- **Hero first:** transform the largest mountain, building, tree mass, slope, or coastline into one broad artificial landform or spatial body.
- **World field second:** transform water, plaza, sand, sky opening, or ground into a designed liquid core, luminous basin, mist field, or calm spatial void.
- **Circuit third:** continuously transform an existing railing, road, shoreline, river, ridge, shadow edge, or building line into one usable route.
- The circuit organizes movement through the world; it is not automatically the largest or most detailed object.
- Keep one hero, one circuit action, and generous quiet space. Avoid scattered rides and decorative props.

## Workflow

1. Read `references/visual-system.md`, `references/transformation-rules.md`, and `references/examples.md`.
2. Inspect the source and write a compact map covering every major visual zone.
3. Decide what is preserved topologically and what is replaced materially.
4. Choose one hero transformation, one field transformation, and one circuit action.
5. Generate with both image inputs using the mandatory reference protocol.
6. Review at full size and thumbnail size. Regenerate if the result remains a photograph with an added object.
7. Deliver the source and result as a visible before/after pair and name the three principal transformations.

## Non-Negotiable Aesthetic

- Translate Chinese aesthetics through layered depth, borrowed scenery, winding approach, void/solid rhythm, and sequential reveal. Do not insert literal pagodas, lanterns, dragons, calligraphy, or unrelated flowers.
- Favor a few broad, legible shapes over filigree and miniature elements.
- Use a fresh, luminous, controlled palette and premium physically coherent materials.
- Colorful means orchestrated color relationships, not five unrelated bright colors.
- Transparent soft-body landforms and liquid volumes may be dominant when derived from the source. Avoid only generic clear-glass futurism, cheap acrylic, and indiscriminate chrome.

## Quality Gate

Approve only when all are true:

- At thumbnail size, the result reads first as a complete new 3D world, not as an edited photograph.
- At least three major zones have new geometry or material identity.
- The source remains recognizable through topology when compared with the before image; literal photographic fidelity is unnecessary.
- The hero landform dominates, the circuit is continuous and usable, and the field has a designed role.
- Every transformed zone traces back to a source zone; no unrelated decoration is required.
- The result feels fresh, clear, emotional, playful, and premium.

Reject immediately when any are true:

- It can be described as “the original photo plus a path, slide, bridge, or installation.”
- Covering the circuit reveals an otherwise unchanged photograph.
- More than roughly one third of the major non-sky surfaces retain their original photographic material identity.
- The route dominates while the surrounding world remains untreated.
- Different sources converge to the same white-and-mint S-curve promenade.
- The scene is dark, cluttered, literal-Chinese, glassy for its own sake, or visually cheap.
