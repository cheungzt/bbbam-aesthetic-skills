# B.B.BAM Shanshui Circuit

Create a new world with a visible lineage to the source. Do not reproduce the photo literally and do not impose one fixed scene template.

## Three-Layer Method

Treat these as independent decisions:

1. **Structural seed — selective fidelity**
   - Extract 3–5 strong source anchors: a ridge silhouette, water opening, foreground growth rhythm, skyline cadence, shore diagonal, railing line, trunk spacing, or roof curve.
   - Preserve their relationship or directional logic, not every object and not exact pixel positions.
   - The result should make viewers say “this came from that photo,” while still feeling substantially invented.

2. **World transformation — high creative freedom**
   - Apply 2–4 operations: enlarge, condense, merge, extrude, suspend, hollow, wrap, liquefy, inflate, connect, or invert scale.
   - Allow meaningful recomposition of depth and scale. A plant field may become a translucent reed colony, soft domes, membrane forest, or glowing growth architecture; a ridge may become an enclosing shell; water may become an interactive liquid core.
   - Every major invented system must originate from a source anchor, but it may change shape, scale, material, and spatial role dramatically.
   - A circuit, path, ball, vehicle, or signal object is optional. Use one only when it strengthens a source-derived idea; never add it automatically.

3. **Surface polish — strict and independent**
   - Keep forms rich and layered, but make every visible surface smooth, continuous, thick, and intentionally modeled.
   - Use long clean highlights, rounded transitions, controlled refraction, coherent seams, and broad connected strata.
   - Remove fragmented surface noise: no pebble-like pieces, cellular chunks, shredded glass, mosaic facets, crystalline rubble, gelatin pellets, repeated tiny blobs, or dense accidental micro-objects.
   - Smoothness means polishing the surface, not simplifying the world, flattening the source, or reducing invention.

## Fixed Visual DNA

- fresh luminous daylight or clean blue-hour air;
- colored translucent soft resin / blown-glass / liquid-soft-body volumes;
- bright cyan, blue, jade, tea green, lilac, warm milk, or source-led clear color;
- contemporary Chinese spatial logic through layered depth, borrowed scenery, winding reveal, void/solid rhythm, and fluid continuity;
- high-end C4D finish with restrained caustics and strong subject hierarchy;
- no dark cyberpunk, literal Chinese symbols, cheap plastic, dirty realism, or ornamental clutter.

## Reference Protocol

Before generating, inspect `assets/shanshui-after.png` and read the reference files.

- **Source image:** supplies structural anchors, semantic cues, and transformation opportunities.
- **Style example:** supplies transformation ambition, spatial invention, material quality, hierarchy, and emotional brightness.
- Do not copy either image literally.
- Do not let source fidelity collapse the result into a filter.
- Do not let the example force a standard lake, loop, ball, mountain, or palette.

## Workflow

1. Read `references/visual-system.md`, `references/transformation-rules.md`, and `references/examples.md`.
2. List 3–5 source anchors and the relationship worth preserving.
3. Write one world thesis explaining how those anchors become a new spatial experience.
4. Choose 2–4 transformation operations.
5. Decide whether an optional movement/signal element earns its place.
6. Generate the whole world.
7. Review twice:
   - **Lineage test:** can the main transformations be traced to the source?
   - **Surface test:** are forms smooth and complete without fragmented chunks?
8. Deliver a before/after pair and name the principal source-to-world mappings.

## Quality Gate

Approve only when:

- the output is clearly a new designed world, not stylized retouching;
- 3–5 source anchors remain traceable through transformed relationships;
- scale, depth, and geometry show meaningful invention;
- one hero dominates and supporting systems enrich it;
- surfaces are smooth, coherent, and premium even when the world is complex;
- optional paths, circuits, balls, or markers have a clear source-derived purpose.

Reject when:

- the result merely redraws the original objects with translucent material;
- exact source layout is treated as a lock rather than a seed;
- a generic lake, loop, white road, or peach sphere appears by habit;
- detail comes from fragmented chunks, cells, pellets, facets, shards, pebbles, or dense tiny objects;
- removing surface fragments also removed the world’s concept, hierarchy, or emotional payoff.
