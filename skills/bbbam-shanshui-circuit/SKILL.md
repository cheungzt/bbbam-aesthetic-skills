# B.B.BAM Shanshui Circuit

Create a new world with visible source lineage. Do not reproduce the photo literally and do not impose one fixed scene template.

## Four Independent Layers

Treat these as separate decisions. Never fix one by weakening another.

1. **Structural seed — selective fidelity**
   - Extract 3–5 strong anchors: ridge silhouette, water opening, foreground growth rhythm, skyline cadence, shore diagonal, railing line, trunk spacing, roof curve, or rock grouping.
   - Preserve their relationship or directional logic, not every object or exact pixel position.
   - Make viewers recognize the ancestry while seeing a substantially invented world.

2. **World transformation — high creative freedom**
   - Apply 2–4 operations: enlarge, condense, merge, extrude, suspend, hollow, wrap, liquefy, inflate, connect, or invert scale.
   - Recompose depth and scale meaningfully. Turn a plant field into a transparent growth colony, soft domes, membrane forest, or glowing architecture; turn a ridge into an enclosing shell; turn water into an interactive liquid core.
   - Derive every major system from a source anchor, but change its shape, scale, material, grouping, and spatial role boldly.
   - Use a circuit, path, ball, vehicle, or signal only when it strengthens a source-derived idea. Never add it automatically.

3. **Form hierarchy — strict detail-scale budget**
   - Build richness from three scales only:
     - **macro:** 1–3 hero systems such as a shell ridge, liquid basin, city canopy, or large growth field;
     - **meso:** grouped domes, column families, terraces, folded masses, or broad internal layers;
     - **accent:** a few long seams, currents, bands, or highlights.
   - Forbid a fourth micro-detail scale. Do not fill gaps with hundreds of leaves, blades, rods, sparkles, dots, bubbles, pebbles, facets, or tiny repeated objects.
   - Condense dense source detail into designed masses. Translate foliage into broad translucent color bodies, layered silhouettes, continuous ribbons, smooth canopy sheets, or a limited number of substantial columns—not leaf-by-leaf reconstruction.
   - Render water as a continuous clean field with a few broad wave bands or one coherent current—not glitter, stipple, confetti highlights, or dense ripples.
   - Keep at least 80% of visible transformed surfaces broad and uninterrupted. Let accents occupy at most 5% of the image.

4. **Surface polish — strict and independent**
   - Keep the full world concept and spatial layering while making every exterior skin smooth, continuous, thick, and intentionally modeled.
   - Use long clean highlights, rounded transitions, controlled refraction, coherent seams, and broad connected strata.
   - Remove fragmented construction: no cellular chunks, shredded glass, mosaic facets, crystalline rubble, gelatin pellets, pebble clusters, repeated tiny blobs, noisy internal debris, or accidental micro-objects.
   - Preserve complexity through overlap, silhouette, scale, depth, and material layering—not through surface noise.
   - Smoothness means polishing and consolidating, not simplifying the world, flattening the source, or turning the result into a filter.

## Fixed Visual DNA

- fresh luminous daylight or clean blue-hour air;
- colored translucent soft resin, blown glass, and liquid-soft-body volumes;
- bright cyan, blue, jade, tea green, lilac, warm milk, or source-led clear color;
- contemporary Chinese spatial logic through layered depth, borrowed scenery, winding reveal, void/solid rhythm, and fluid continuity;
- high-end C4D finish with restrained caustics and strong subject hierarchy;
- no dark cyberpunk, literal Chinese symbols, cheap plastic, dirty realism, or ornamental clutter.

## Reference Protocol

Before generating, inspect `assets/shanshui-after.png` and read all reference files.

- Let the source supply structural anchors, semantic cues, and transformation opportunities.
- Let the style example supply transformation ambition, spatial invention, hierarchy, emotional brightness, and material quality.
- Do not copy either image literally.
- Do not let source fidelity collapse the result into a filter.
- Do not let the example force a standard lake, loop, ball, mountain, or palette.
- Treat fine foliage, grass, waves, windows, crowds, and urban texture as raw density to condense, not detail to reproduce.

## Workflow

1. Read `references/visual-system.md`, `references/transformation-rules.md`, and `references/examples.md`.
2. List 3–5 source anchors and the relationship worth preserving.
3. Write one world thesis that turns those anchors into a new spatial experience.
4. Choose 2–4 transformation operations.
5. Plan macro, meso, and accent scales before prompting.
6. Decide whether an optional movement or signal element earns its place.
7. Generate the whole world.
8. Review four tests:
   - **Lineage:** trace the main transformations to the source.
   - **Invention:** verify meaningful changes in scale, depth, grouping, and spatial role.
   - **Surface:** verify smooth complete skins without fragmented construction.
   - **Thumbnail:** view at 15–20% scale; reject any area that becomes leafy, glittery, granular, stippled, spiky, or busy.
9. Deliver a before/after pair and name the principal source-to-world mappings.

## Quality Gate

Approve only when:

- the output is clearly a new designed world, not stylized retouching;
- 3–5 source anchors remain traceable through transformed relationships;
- scale, depth, and geometry show meaningful invention;
- one hero dominates and meso systems support it;
- visual complexity comes from organized layers and large-to-medium forms;
- broad smooth surfaces dominate every region;
- optional paths, circuits, balls, or markers have a clear source-derived purpose.

Reject when:

- the result merely redraws the original objects with translucent material;
- exact source layout is treated as a lock rather than a seed;
- a generic lake, loop, white road, or peach sphere appears by habit;
- transparent domes contain individually rendered leaves or tangled plant noise;
- water contains dense pinprick highlights or tiny repeated ripples;
- detail comes from cells, pellets, facets, shards, pebbles, rods, blades, sparkles, or repeated tiny objects;
- smoothing removed the world’s concept, hierarchy, source lineage, or emotional payoff.
